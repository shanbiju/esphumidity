2. Wi-Fi & Hardware Pin Configuration Non-Volatile NVS
📶 Connecting to other Wi-Fi networks:
1. Method 1 (WebSerial USB): Keep USB plugged in and click "Send Wi-Fi Config via USB" below.
2. Method 2 (Phone / Hotspot): If your ESP32 cannot find Wi-Fi, it broadcasts an open hotspot named ESP32-Climate-Setup. Connect to it on your phone or laptop and visit http://192.168.4.1 to scan and select any Wi-Fi network!
